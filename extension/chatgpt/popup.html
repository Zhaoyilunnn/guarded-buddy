What gets archived?

Conversation JSON is sent to your local Buddy receiver. Markdown contains visible text on the current branch. Attachment files are not downloaded.

Deleting a ChatGPT conversation does not delete its archive. A cloud client may replicate the destination directory; Buddy cannot confirm cloud upload completion.
